Spellflight

Burn the source inside the dark ball. Three bursts of light, then a spirit broom through a starlit grove.

Choose three cards

Each card lends one spell to the beam. Tap a card to take it or set it down.

How to play

Stage 1 — the source. Aim with a finger, the mouse, the arrows, or a stick. The ball is made of dark pieces. The source is the knot of bad energy inside. Hold the beam on it until it burns through. Do that three times. Keys 1, 2, and 3 cast your cards' spells. A gamepad's west, north, and east buttons do the same.

Stage 2 — the grove. The view sits at the rider's eyes, so the hands, the handle, and the front of the broom stay in the lower part of the screen. Steer along the path. Shoot cosmic bolts with a finger, the space bar, or the Shoot button. Dodge trees and low logs. Demons drift in the lane. Press C or View for a close chase camera. Reach the ring of light.

P or Escape pauses. M mutes. Sound is generated in the browser.
